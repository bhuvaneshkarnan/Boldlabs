Frequently Asked Questions (Landing Page FAQ Section)

Answer every prospect hesitation upfront to maximize landing page conversions.

Q1: Can I use my existing clinic phone number?

Yes. You can connect your existing WhatsApp Business number. If you prefer to keep your personal WhatsApp separate, you can activate a new dedicated SIM card or virtual number for your clinic in under 15 minutes.

Q2: Why doesn't the Starter Plan (₹999) come with CRM login credentials?

By design! Many doctors and solo clinic owners don't have time to sit in front of a computer or remember software passwords. Starter is built for extreme convenience: the AI answers your WhatsApp inquiries 24/7, and instantly sends structured booking alerts directly to your personal WhatsApp. You get 100% of the automation with 0% software headache.

Q3: What if a patient asks a medical or complex question the AI doesn't know?

Our AI is trained with strict safety boundaries. It answers business FAQs (timings, doctors, procedures, pricing, directions) flawlessly. For clinical diagnosis or custom inquiries, it politely states that the doctor/staff will follow up, and instantly sends you an alert with the patient's chat history so you can step in seamlessly.

Q4: How do the WhatsApp Appointment Reminders work in the Standard Plan?

When an appointment is scheduled in your Web CRM, the system automatically schedules two WhatsApp reminders: one 24 hours prior and one 2 hours prior. The patient receives an interactive WhatsApp message with quick-tap [Confirm Appointment] and [Reschedule] buttons. Your CRM pipeline updates status instantly in real time.

Q5: Are there any hidden WhatsApp or Meta API charges?

No hidden surprises. For the Starter Plan, customer-initiated conversations fall within Meta's free service window. For Standard and Pro plans, outbound utility templates (reminders and review prompts) adhere to Meta's official Indian business rates (~₹0.15 to ₹0.35 per delivered utility message). We connect directly to the official Meta Cloud API for guaranteed 100% compliance.

Q6: Can I upgrade from Starter to Standard later?

Yes, seamlessly! Start with the ₹999 plan. When you're ready to add receptionist logins, Kanban pipeline tracking, and automated reminders, you can upgrade with 1-click inside your billing panel without changing your phone number or bot settings.
